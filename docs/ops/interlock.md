# Interlock operations

Package 0.0.11 adds live core controls. Protocol/content version 1 and
world schema 3 remain compatible. The server owns every state change;
the door sends keys and displays the resulting views.

In a core, press E to inspect its controls and choose a flip. The menu
shows the current state, declared reset and three-second action. Damage
interrupts the channel. A committed flip adds 10 base trace and posts to
the physical zone and the runner. A failed save changes neither the
machine nor the trace balance. Occupied or unsafe door closures wait for
clear floor and a return route, and say so in the view.

Ordinary effects last their declared 5-30 minutes. Named mission,
lighting, vestibule and transit exceptions retain their shorter limits.
Only an explicitly eligible noncritical effect may persist for its live
relay owner. Expiry and ownership changes remove their own effects;
other live controls and event geometry remain. S21 supplies live relay
assignments. A tram hold affects its named platform, adds at most 30
seconds to departure, then enforces 120 seconds of immunity. Trips
already underway finish normally.

Stand beside hardware and press E. The menu discloses the five-second
channel, 100 shock and ten-minute outage before offering Cut power.
Choose C and then Y; Esc or B leaves without starting a cut. Any damage,
movement or loss of the working body interrupts it. A cut checks the
initiator and every affected physical body before starting and again at
completion. A protected runner refuses the entire cut. NPC feedback
consent never permits player hardware harm. Safe hardware also requires
its scoped work-order permit. The Wake lesson has no cuttable hardware.

On success, the affected runners return to their physical bodies and
the entrance dims to `[C?]`. Entering is refused until power returns.
Offline state and all affected shock records commit together. A runner
already in the Silt can still rise to the outward district if its
original core loses power. The Shaft's two commissioned cabinets refer
to one offline deadline; its actual district geometry arrives in S25.

Private-core integration is available to S18's rental service; no
apartments are offered for rent in S15. Each commissioned apartment has
one entrance, its own tier 1-3 core, hardware and delivery vestibule.
Passkey opens only that vestibule for 30 seconds. The manifest shows
unsecured storage units, never bank balances, secured goods or impound.
A lift transfers one unit, splitting a stack if necessary. The apartment
has one allowance per rolling 24 hours across all attackers. The unit,
shared receipt and dated resident record commit together. Residents see
a notification and can scroll their last 20 intrusion records. Item
details remain readable at both terminal sizes.

The shipped ten-zone hub remains the live city. Future cores, transit
machines and service layouts are staged until their real maps and
bindings exist. Pumps use authored layouts with five seconds warning;
S19 selects the frozen work-order allocation and its 20-minute expiry.
Camera effects concern mission records and never blind combat detection.
Powered NPC attacks and machinery damage retain the initiating body and
recheck safety when the hit settles.

An armed machine does not grant anonymous offline attacks. If the
initiating body becomes protected, ineligible, or leaves the world, its
player-attributed damage is suppressed even while the visible machine
effect remains active. Ordinary NPC behaviour resumes when the control
effect expires. Until S20 supplies faction-channel membership, owner
reports also use the slice's hall-zone fallback: everyone physically in
that hall can hear the report, including an exact Shiv body location.
S20 replaces that interim audience with the owning faction's channel.

Interlock records live in server-owned world meta and use the existing
Commerce transaction boundary. Back up the world through the service's
normal storage procedure. Never edit its database while the server runs.
An override of a zone needs both its `.json` and `.map` in content.local.
Validation refuses missing control targets, unbound powered actors,
uncommissioned layouts and ownership persistence for service denial.

Validation uses deterministic headless scenarios and real NetBBS native
stdio probes. `scripts/accept_s15_netbbs.py` has `controls` and `private`
stages, each supporting 80x24 and 132x50. It creates isolated worlds and
nodes. The control fixture commissions a tier-three Civic core without
ICE and labels the plaza unsafe; private acceptance supplies an S18
rental/storage fixture and a Passkey loan. It grants no funds, grade,
skills or placement. Real walking, scheduled transit, program keys,
menus, transactions and return to NetBBS remain exercised. S14's separate
probes own combat/ICE acceptance. S25 still owns actual Gate Nine
geography, all deferred commerce/core anchors and the crowded HUD gate.
