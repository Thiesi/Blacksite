# Combat and recovery (S10)

Tab selects a visible eligible target; Shift-Tab cycles backwards. F
fires. The first new threat gives both callers a two-second warning;
press F again to commit. The warning is a chance to move to safety or
cover. Continued exchanges use the weapon's cooldown, shown on the hint
line. X switches between equipped firearms and melee; 1 uses the first
patch by default. Inventory's slot chooser binds other consumables.

V opens abilities. Enter uses the selected ability; 1-5 binds it to a
quick key. Cantors start with tier-one access. Higher tiers require the
listed installed resonance implant. Hymns affect self until S22 supplies
local crew recipients; Ninefold needs a downed crew member and does not
spend its cooldown when nobody can be picked up. Movement, damage and
disconnect interrupt a cast. Cooldowns begin after completion. Z toggles
Ghost stealth; firing breaks it. W and vi movement retain their bindings.

Safe and pocket bodies reject damage, including delayed chemical harm
and secondary targets. Contested PvP requires both callers to be grade
five. Open zones permit under-grade combat. Attacking a neutral eligible
caller in contested ground makes the attacker Marked for fifteen minutes,
including on a miss; attacking a Marked caller is exempt. S20 supplies
the full faction/Marked consequences, and S11 supplies Warden pursuit.

Health zero leaves a body down for five seconds. A nearby non-hostile
caller can press E to pick it up at 25% health; its attacker cannot.
Otherwise the body enters a twenty-second decant. The clone charge is
5% of hand chits plus grade times ten. Hand funds pay first; unpaid debt
persists on the BBS identity. Fade removes 2% of XP above the current
grade's threshold, without removing a grade or legal status. Banked
funds are untouched. Future earned awards service debt; transfers do not.

Unsecured equipment drops in contested/open ground for five minutes.
The victim and killer have immediate access; others wait sixty seconds.
Secured gear, installed implants, journal records and bound recovery
goods stay with the character. Down, decant, cooldown, temporary effect
and cache deadlines survive a service restart. A combat sleeper who
survives the sixty-second logout interval returns to Wake Hall with the
clone charge and fade, keeping carried gear. It is not a free escape.

Vats supply missing recovery gear when usable weapons are gone: bound
sidearm, fifty rounds, coverall and two patches. Only missing quantities
are replaced. Bound goods cannot be sold or dropped. Rechargeable cells
remain as zero-charge containers when spent, so chargers still work.
Press E at a public vat for missing supplies even without dying. It
replaces only missing quantities; repeated requests cannot stockpile them.
New starter coveralls begin secured. Bound loan coveralls need no free
secured slot, so a full set of protected items cannot block recovery.

The corridor drone is private to each caller. It cannot kill, create
debt, drop loot or affect another caller. Tab/F teaches the combat rhythm;
B skips. Walking out also remains possible. The later private Lattice
tutorial belongs to S13/S14.

Combat uses the zone's seeded RNG. The catalogue's timing estimates are
models, not evidence that human combat is fun. Deterministic duels and
real terminal acceptance validate rules and controls; S29 still owns
measured human pacing, cover, retreat and earning-route playtests.

## Consumer handoff

S11 uses the shared legality/harm/shot boundary for NPC combat. S13/S14
must preserve safe physical bodies and indirect initiator attribution.
S16 applies progression to the same formulas and earned-chit settlement.
S20 supplies faction relations, actual membership and faction vats. S22
supplies server-owned local recipients to `Combat.allies_provider` and
crew pickup permissions. The existing S25 commerce activation gate stays
in force when maps arrive.
