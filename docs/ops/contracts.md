# Contracts and service work orders

Q opens accepted jobs. At a contract board, E shows offers and terms.
The Tin Halo brokers generated faction commissions; they can be returned
there or at the commissioning faction's board. The standing reward still
belongs to that faction. Vesper's board also offers the first parcel job.
At Vesper's counter, V opens the shop from its contract card.

Choose a solo or crew proposal, read its terms, then confirm. Each listed
crew member consents. The committed roster owns the objectives and reward
scale; later invitations, kicks or leadership changes do not add a payee.
Take part directly or provide nearby support. Merely being listed or
arriving for payment gives no participation credit.

Generated jobs refresh every 30 minutes and expire 60 minutes after
acceptance. A character has three active generated slots, including
completed jobs awaiting payment. Story jobs do not use those slots and
have no overall expiry. An explicitly labelled field leg can have its own
deadline. Its current countdown appears in the HUD and Q card.

Use J in the Q panel for the evidence journal and D for retired jobs.
Records separate observation, the source's claim, the open question and
next action. P reads optional source text. Reading it is never an answer
quiz or a condition of receiving the record.

Supplies belong to the accepted job and cannot be sold or transferred.
If an original worker leaves with a required supply, Q names the earlier
pickup or lift that another original worker can use. These replacements
do not repeat earlier objectives or create stock for sale. Physical and
core planting consume the supply only after the saved three-second action.

W withdraws participation. An original worker can use R to resume a
generated/story job under its original terms, while its deadline permits.
Withdrawal clears that worker's prior participation credit. Retrying a
failed stage preserves earlier completed stages; a timed field failure
can return to a disclosed launch checkpoint. A public repair withdrawal
is final for that reservation. Disconnecting preserves its progress only
until the original reservation deadline.

Publication decisions belong to each character. The card shows its exact
standing, reward and temporary access effect. A previous outcome cannot
be collected again through another crew. A veteran can help a new worker
for zero additional story reward without rewriting the veteran's choice.

## Public service orders

Services fault on server uptime, not by replaying an offline backlog.
An order reserves ten minutes; its completed allocation lasts twenty.
The chosen and foregone allocations appear before consent. The last
verified repair action saves allocation and fixed worker rewards together.
There is no second payment to collect at a handler.

Each participating worker receives 200 chits, 100 XP and +5 standing with
the handler faction, without crew scaling. Ordinary source XP may compose
with this award. The Operator's ordinary chit bonus applies last.

| Service | Common | Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| Sump | dry public shortcut | workers' salvage niche and two shared power units |
| Cold Chain | visitors' installation service fee -10% | workers' service fee -20% |
| Depot | public surplus buy-back cap x2 | workers' cache, two shared power units |
| Relay Zero | current local NPC/ICE patrol route | temporary credential for its declared gate |

The two salvage units belong to the whole order, not to each worker.
Depot caps preserve spending through expiry and restart. Cold Chain
changes only the installation service fee and applies the strongest
eligible discount once. Essential care, food, water, uplinks and outward
travel remain available. Follow the signed return before water changes.

S19 ships Cold Chain on the clinic map. The other service locations and
Season 1 chains remain inactive until the complete maps and dialogue are
integrated. See the [content handoff](../plan/s19-content-handoff.md) for
operator/development readiness and the [earning report](s19-earnings.md)
for the distinction between receipt amounts and assumed session pacing.
