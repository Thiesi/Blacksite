# S21 relay consumers and full-city acceptance

Reference: [S21](slices/S21-relays-and-territory.md),
[PR #38](https://github.com/Thiesi/Blacksite/pull/38) and
[NetBBS #56](https://github.com/Thiesi/Blacksite/issues/56).

The two live anchors are Cold Chain and Block. Fourteen definitions,
cores and small curated member counters remain authoring content until
S25 supplies and validates their real physical maps. Shaft additionally
requires S26's level-opening provider. No fixture proves those districts.

`core.relays.benefit(player, kind, zone)` returns the canonical numeric
amount only for a commissioned relay owned by that player's faction.
Check `is not None`: a telemetry flag intentionally has amount zero.
Ownership, membership, location and level opening are checked live.
Never cache permission in an event proposal or a menu quote.

S24 binds the shared `Factions.curfew(zone)` provider. Relay claims read
that same provider: new claims are refused and existing clocks pause
without losing progress. Public work-order repair remains independent.
Event expiry removes only its own overlay, not relay/core/service state.

S26 uses the exact relay ledger cutoff and season reset in its committed
season settlement. Neutralize ownership and persistent owned core effects,
reset influence/fractions and abort live claims. Retain earned treasury,
daily identity receipts and rolling 24-hour capture reward limits. A
failed season settlement must not publish reset ownership or rewards.

S25 explicitly tags only the real optional cable/outbound gate exits with
their relay IDs. Validate that the cable has an independent public free
route and Gate Nine retains ordinary free outbound openings and inward
pedestrian egress. A paid pass survives a stop before departure and is
consumed by live or restored arrival; failed payment saves charge nothing.
Ownership changes refresh the quote rather than charging a prior free
choice. The labelled passage fixture changes existing hub exits only;
it is not proof of the final geography or travel pacing.

The S25/S26 human play gate also measures unopposed relay idle time,
useful decisions during contested holds, solo travel/earning time and
reasons to return on another evening. The three/eight-minute rules remain
canon pending those measurements. If full-city play exposes a boring
wait or an affordability trap, change canon, tests and dependent issues
together. Automated accelerated fixtures cannot close this human gate.

S28 retains authenticated, audited `relay list|set`; neutralization and
operator ownership changes invent no captures or XP. S31 may consume
committed attributed capture facts under the existing optional host
publication policy. Login, forecasts and operator changes publish none.

S23 now explicitly depends on S21's single faction treasury authority.
Its Kestrel fee joins the same source receipt as bank settlement and
personal impound delivery. Fees increase treasury only, never season
influence. A separate faction balance would lose fees at the next relay
checkpoint or pay a daily share from the wrong balance. S28's operator
audit and S29/S30/S31 integration acceptance must retain this invariant.
