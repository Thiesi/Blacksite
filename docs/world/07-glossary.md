# Blacksite — Glossary

Every proper noun and term of art in `00-bible.md` and
`../design/00-game-design.md`, one or two lines each, with its source.
Add a term here whenever you introduce one anywhere else (bible §13).

---

**Achebe-Roux, Tamsin** — Unmoored recruiter. (factions)

**Achterberg, Sunny** — Kestrel recruiter and yard foreman. (factions)

**Adebayo, Sgt. P.** — a Warden night-watch sergeant at the Core precinct. (found texts)

**Adeyemi, Rook** — Ferrymen Charter of the outer routes. (factions)

**Adjei, Dr. Corvin** — Sablier Chief Medical Officer, faction leader. (factions)

**Apartment** — Rented housing in the Terraces: storage, a safe, a
terminal and a private core. Rent lapses to eviction. (design §8.7)

**Archetype** — The Sablier template a Wake was decanted from: Hardline,
Ghost, Cantor or Operator. Sets starting attributes and one unique
ability. (bible §8.1, design §3.2)

**Ashgrove, Commander Ruth** — head of Halvard Spire Security. (factions)

**Attributes** — Frame, Nerve, Cortex, Resonance, Vitals; 1 to 100,
raised through skill lines and grade. (design §3.1)

**Balance weight** — the probability adjustment applied to CUST/TEN- tagged
lines by the season's Custodian/Tenant balance. (personae)

**band** — one of the five layers of the Silt. (lattice)

**Banked chits** — Chits held at a Kestrel branch or an apartment safe;
never lost to death. (design §8.1)

**Bark** — an idle line spoken to the zone log. (personae)

**The beacon line** — the Ferrymen's lit route through Beacon Ring. (gazetteer)

**Black clinic** — A Sink surgery that installs implants at 60 % of list
with a 15 % failure rate and no questions. (design §8.5)

**Black core** — A syndicate core; at trace 100 its owners learn exactly
where your body is. (design §6.3)

**Black ICE** — ICE that reaches back up the link and damages the runner's
body. (bible §5.1, design §6.3)

**Blacksite level** — The instanced seasonal raid zone and sector that
opens when the Depth meter fills. (design §13)

**Blacksite, the** — Site Zero's popular and now official name: the
sealed installation under the Spire. Also the game. (bible §3.3, §4.6)

**Block Street** — the Terraces' main street. (gazetteer)

**Bounty** — Chits posted on a player's name on the market board;
collected by whoever kills them outside a safe zone. Refused below
grade 5. (design §8.3)

**Brann, Marshal Odile** — Head of the Wardens; has kept the Core safe for
nineteen years by any means. (bible §8.2)

**Burning** — Attacking another runner in the Lattice with programs.
(design §6.1)

**Cable Boss** — Red Sable title held by Yusra Demir. (factions)

**Cantillations** — Choir program items for the rig. (factions)

**Cantor** — Archetype decanted from a template Sablier will not discuss;
the only one for whom resonance implants work. (bible §8.1)

**Cell** — A node in a Lattice sector: public, gated, hidden, core
entrance or Silt descent. (bible §5.1, design §6.1)

**The chair** — the empty seat in the Tramyard dispatch office. (story arcs)

**Chapel Foundry** — the Choir's maker mark for resonance implants. (catalog)

**Chapel Ward** — The Choir's district: repurposed cooling towers, quiet
and well kept. (bible §4.3)

**Chits** — Money. Kestrel ration scrip that became currency. (bible §9)

**Choir Surge** — World event: a reported signal surge, bounded ICE variants, Cantors
are stronger, the Silt is briefly mapped. (bible §10, design §12)

**Choir, the Ninefold** — Technognostic order in Chapel Ward that hears the
Tenant through implants. (bible §3.4, §7)

**Chorister** — Responsive Silt ICE with a visible sampling procedure; its
allegiance is unknown. (lattice)

**Chronicle, the** — Read-only in-game history the server appends with
each season's outcome. (bible §11, design §13)

**Civic Pattern** — Warden standard issue; Halvard designs in blue. (catalog)

**Clone debt** — The cost of dying: a share of chits on hand, a fee by
grade, and a fade of XP. (design §5.4)

**Cold Chain Relay, Depot Relay, Gate Relay Nine, Cable Relay, Sump Relay,
Bell Relay, Relay Zero, Yard Relay, Block Relay, Cavern Relay, Outworks
Relay, Shaft Relay, Mile Relay, Beacon Relay, Crash Relay, Far Relay** — the
sixteen relays. (gazetteer)

**Containment charge** — Halvard item that cuts a core's power from outside its room. (factions)

**Contested zone** — A district where player-versus-player combat is
allowed under faction relations and the Marked rule. (bible §4.3,
design §5.1)

**Contract** — A task from a faction handler or Vesper: fetch, hack,
escort, clear, plant or survey. (design §9)

**Contractor Street** — Old Works' main street. (gazetteer)

**Controls** — Objects in a core mapped to meatspace objects: doors,
turrets, cameras, vats, trams. (bible §5.1, design §6.2)

**Convoy** — World event: a Kestrel convoy crosses the Scour; escort it or
raid it. (bible §10, design §12)

**Core (Lattice)** — A fortified system inside a sector: rooms holding
data, controls and ICE, with a tier and a physical hardware location.
(bible §5.1, design §6.2)

**Core, the (district)** — Downtown Karst: the safe zone under the Civic
Authority, the social hub. (bible §4.2)

**Corpse cache** — Unsecured inventory dropped at the death tile for five
minutes in contested and open zones. (design §5.4)

**Cortex** — Attribute for Lattice speed, program strength, trace
resistance and crafting. (design §3.1)

**Cover** — A tile value 0 to 3 that reduces to-hit for a target adjacent
to it. (design §5.3)

**Crew** — A party of two to five players with shared chat, list, beacon
and loot rules. (design §10)

**Curfew** — World event: the Wardens restrict inbound Core gates for an
hour; outward travel remains open. (bible §10, design §12)

**Curfew pass** — Warden item permitting Core entry during Curfew. (factions)

**Custodian, the** — The official designation of the machine mind under
the Blacksite; procedural, patient, programmed to reconnect. (bible §6)

**Cut power** — Reaching a core's hardware in meatspace and jacking out
every runner inside; the core goes offline for ten minutes. (design §6.4)

**Data** — Loot inside a core room: chits, salvage data, contract
objectives, season contributions. (design §6.2)

**Day count** — the Wake's running tally toward day ninety, shown by Sablier staff. (personae)

**Deep** — Unmoored +90 rank. (factions)

**Demir, Yusra** — Red Sable's Cable Boss. (factions)

**Depot 4** — Kestrel's Tramyard ration depot. (found texts)

**Depth meter** — The city-wide season counter fed by contributions;
filling it opens the Blacksite level. (bible §11, design §13)

**Depth survey / routing survey / listening survey** — the per-season
Descent-console flavour for contributions. (story arcs)

**descent cell** — the one cell per sector that leads to the Silt. (lattice)

**Descent console** — The object at every faction hall where season
contributions are delivered. (design §13)

**Descent, the** — The seasonal arc: the city digs one level deeper into
the Blacksite each season. (bible §11, design §13)

**Detox** — Sablier's cure for dependency. (catalog)

**Dispatcher, the** — Kestrel's voice on every convoy channel; never met,
possibly a Custodian fragment. (bible §8.2)

**Dissonance** — Cantor nerve-jam attacks, and the damage type they deal.
(design §3.2, §5.3)

**Dorn** — a Kestrel ration clerk of the Long Quiet, Depot 4. (found texts)

**Down** — Health 0: five seconds during which a crew member can pick you
up before you are dead. (design §5.4)

**Drone** — An Operator-controlled actor with its own position, health and
one weapon or tool. (bible §9, design §5.3)

**The Drop** — the Unmoored basement where Ninety-Nine's dead drop lives. (gazetteer)

**The Drowned Level**, **Karst Caverns**, **Blacksite Outworks**, **Shaft
Foot** — Undercity zones below the Service Level. (gazetteer)

**Drugs** — Effective, addictive on a per-substance clock, with a crash.
(bible §9, design §8.6)

**Evasion** — Nerve / 2 plus cover; subtracted from to-hit. (design §3.1)

**Event** — A scheduled or admin-forced world happening: Ring-fall,
Curfew, Choir Surge, Exhale, Convoy. (design §12)

**Exhale** — World event: the Blacksite breathes and the Undercity fills
with warm air, old data and things from below. (bible §3.5, §10)

**Fabrication** — Turning salvage plus a schematic into an item at a
workshop; also the Cortex skill line for it. (design §8.4)

**Faction** — One of eight joinable powers, or Freelance. (bible §7,
design §7)

**Fade** — The XP lost on death: 2 % of progress to the next grade.
(design §5.4)

**The far-side road** — the one-tile road in the fog past the plateau's
edge, opened in Season 3. (story arcs)

**Fennimore, Doc** — Red Sable black-clinic chief. (factions)

**Ferreira, Cass** — speaker of the Unmoored Quorum. (factions)

**Ferry, the** — Title of the Ferrymen's matriarch; Anouk is the eleventh.
(bible §8.2)

**Ferry-make** — anything the Ferrymen build from Ring-fall. (catalog)

**Ferrymen, the** — Scour salvage clans led by the Ferry; hold the outer
relays and the roads. (bible §3.3, §7)

**fragment** — the text residue of a runner who died in the Silt. (lattice)

**Frame** — Attribute for melee and kinetic weapons, carry weight and
armour. (design §3.1)

**Freelance** — Belonging to no faction: walk anywhere a pass allows,
capture nothing, pay full price. (bible §7)

**Gate Nine** — the Tramyard wall gate; Kestrel runs it. (gazetteer)

**Ghost** — Archetype decanted from a Halvard reconnaissance template;
fast, quiet, born with a native rig. (bible §8.1)

**going down** — jacking in. (lattice)

**Grade** — The character level, 1 to 30, from cumulative XP; each grade
gives grade points and Vitals. (design §3.4)

**Grade points** — Spent on skill lines; three per grade. (design §3.3)

**Halvard Ordnance / Ostrom Optical** — Halvard subsidiaries that make weapons and optics. (catalog)

**Halvard, Director Anselm** — Grandson of the founder; believes the
concrete over the shaft keeps Karst alive. (bible §8.2)

**Halvard-Ostrom Dynamics** — The corporate-military contractor that built
Site Zero and now holds the Spire. (bible §3.1, §7)

**Hardline** — Archetype decanted from a Site Zero security template;
heavy frame, heavy weapons. (bible §8.1)

**Hass, Wendel** — Civic Authority administrator. (factions)

**Heat (Warden)** — Warden attention on an attacker in a safe zone;
persists ten minutes. (design §5.5)

**Heavy weapon** — a weapon class only Hardlines can carry. (catalog)

**Hound** — hunter ICE spawned at trace 100 in a corporate core. (lattice)

**Hunter** — ICE that spawns at trace 100 in a corporate core and follows
the runner across cells. (design §6.3)

**Hymn** — A Cantor crew buff affecting members within six tiles. (design
§3.2, §5.3)

**ICE** — Defensive software of a core; nobody remembers what the letters
stood for. (bible §5.1, design §6.3)

**Iloh, Sister Nadezhda** — Choir recruiter, keeper of the fragment library. (factions)

**Implant** — Sablier or black-clinic hardware installed under surgery
risk, limited by tolerance and slot. (bible §9, design §8.5)

**Impound** — Kestrel's storage for evicted tenants' goods. (catalog)

**Influence** — Hourly income to a faction from a held relay; the season
leaderboard score. (design §7.4)

**Integrity** — A runner's health analog in the Lattice, 100; at 0, forced
jack-out. (design §6.1)

**Interlock** — The rule that a core action changes a meatspace object
immediately, and a meatspace action can cut a core. (bible §5.3,
design §6.4)

**Jack in / jack out** — Entering and leaving the Lattice at a terminal or
through a rig. (bible §5.2)

**Junction 99** — the hidden Old Works sector cell holding the Ninety-Nine dead drop. (found texts)

**Karst** — The city: four million people on a limestone plateau, sealed
for forty-one years. (bible §1)

**Karst Civic Authority** — The nominal government; its police are the
Wardens. (bible §7)

**Kestrel Issue** — Kestrel's cheap standard-issue gear line. (catalog)

**Kestrel Logistics** — Former Halvard subsidiary that runs trams,
rations, wall gates and Scour convoys. (bible §3.1, §7)

**Kettle, Jory** — Ferrymen recruiter. (factions)

**Kill-switch beacon** — Halvard's Season 3 device at the gallery entrance. (story arcs)

**Kin** — Ferrymen +90 rank. (factions)

**The Lamplighter** — the Undercity's lamp-keeper; nobody knows who pays him. (personae)

**The Landing** — the Ferrymen's camp. (gazetteer)

**Landing, the** — The Ferrymen's base outside the wall. (bible §7)

**Lassiter, Dr. Emeric** — Halvard Archivist. (factions)

**Lattice, the** — Karst's surviving local network; the game's second
layer. Runners say "down". (bible §2, §5)

**Ledger week** — Year 33, when the Unmoored published Red Sable's
subscription ledger; eleven collectors died. (personae)

**LH-A, LH-B** — the two terrestrial long-haul links Karst had; both cut at

**Lindqvist, Petra** — Kestrel Director of Operations, faction leader. (factions)


**Listening** — The Resonance skill line for finding hidden cells and

**The Listening Gallery** — Blacksite Level 3: nine chairs around a column. (story arcs)

**Long Quiet, the** — Years 1 to 20 after the Severance, when the wall

**The Long Sled** — Bram's salvage sled, and by extension the Ferrymen's

**The Long Stair** — the stair from Tramyard Market to the Rim. (gazetteer)

**"looked after"** — Sable term for a door under protection. (found texts)

**Maalouf, T.** — Halvard uplink engineer on shift the night of the

texts)

**Marash, Precentor Ivo** — Choir master of implants. (factions)

**Marchetti, Inspector Dov** — Warden precinct commander. (factions)

in both directions. (bible
§4.3, design §7.3)

**Market board** — The player-to-player market and bounty list, browsable

**Marrow, Sel** — Ferrymen salvage-mistress. (factions)

**Meatspace** — The physical city, as opposed to the Lattice. (design §1)

**Meridian Plaza** — the Core's central plaza. (gazetteer)

**Native rig** — the built-in rig every Ghost is decanted with. (catalog)

**The Neon Stair** — the stair from the Core down to Sodium Row. (gazetteer)

**Nerve** — Attribute for reflexes, evasion, stealth, drones and energy
weapons. (design §3.1)

**Ninety-Day** - legal discharge at grade 5 or after ninety real days,
whichever comes first. Allows apartment rental and faction membership;
contested-zone grade protection is a separate rule. (design section 3.4)

**Ninety-Nine** — A residual: the recording of a runner who went into the

**Ninth** — Choir +90 rank; available to every qualifying member. (factions)

**The Ninth Listener** — Season 3; the listening gallery. (story arcs)

**Okonkwo-Reyes, Marta** — head of Sablier's template vault; Tomasz Reyes's sister. (factions)

**Old Works** — The original contractor town, half-abandoned; the

**Old Works Collective** — the Unmoored's maker mark for rigs and programs. (catalog)

**The one under** — Ferrymen term for the thing in the Blacksite. (personae)

**Open zone** — The Undercity and the Scour: anything goes, nothing marks

**Operator** — Archetype decanted from a logistics template; drones,

**ORB-LINK-1/2/3** — Karst's three orbital uplinks, lost in the cascade. (found texts)

**Osei, Halim** — Kestrel Wall Marshal. (factions)

**The Outworks Door** — Blacksite Level 1: the door in the concrete that is

**the patch frame** — the physical cross-connect where the long-haul links

**Pocket** — A safe area inside a contested zone, such as the Wake Hall or

**Presence** — A runner's manifestation in the Lattice. (design §6.1)

**Program** — Software loaded in a rig slot: attack, shield, decoy,

**Quaye, Sergeant Ama** — Warden recruiter. (factions)

**Quell, Mother Serafine** — Hierophant of the Ninefold Choir. (bible

**Quiet, the** — The self-propagating fault that partitioned the global

**Quorum** — the Unmoored's rotating decision body; also its +70 rank. (factions)

**the Reach, the Floor, the Conduit** — Silt bands 3, 4, and 5. (lattice)

**Recovery** — Halvard's term for a Scour salvage patrol. (personae)

**Red Sable** — The Sink's syndicate: drugs, black market, protection.

**Relations matrix** — The table of hostile, neutral and allied stances

**Relay** — A capturable control point with a terminal and a core in a

design §7.4)

**Renner** — Halvard day supervisor who physically severed the long-haul

**Residual** — A fragment of a past runner persisting in the Silt.

**Residual fragment** — Unmoored single-use program item carrying a past runner's skill. (factions)

**Resonance** — Attribute for resonance implants, Tenant contact and ICE

design §3.1)

**Reyes, Tomasz ("Sable")** — Runs Red Sable from a cable car that never

**Rig** — A Lattice deck, as an implant or a portable; holds program

**The Rim**, **The Floor** — top and bottom of the Sink. (gazetteer)

**Ring, the** — The debris band across the sky since the Severance.

**Ring-fall** — Debris coming down over the Scour; the world event and the

**Row pass** — Red Sable pass for an optional back-room shortcut; public

**Silt beacon** — Choir item marking a descent cell for a crew. (factions)

**Sodium Row** — Entertainment district and neutral ground, enforced by

**Spire, the** — Halvard's arcology over the sealed shaft; a safe zone

## Operational terms

**Allocation** - a work order's bounded public-route or worker-benefit
choice, visible with its expiry. (design section 7.5)

**Cold-chain work order** - restore a clinic's surplus cooling circuit;
ordinary decants remain available. (design section 7.5)

**Depot work order** - reconcile a consignment and surplus dispatch;
minimum food and water never depend on it. (design section 7.5)

**Evidence journal** - observed facts, attributed claims, practical
objectives and consequences, recorded separately. (design section 9.3)

**Expedition** - persistent roster of one to five for a Blacksite run,
independent of the temporary social crew. (design section 13.1)

**Listened** - optional, reversible calibration after the Name choice;
a measured effect, no proof of identity. (design section 13.3)

**Public service work order** - reserve, repair and allocate a surplus
service through physical and Lattice actions. (design section 7.5)

**Receipt** - persistent proof of a completed action and its one-time
reward or branch choice. (design section 9.3)

**Relay Zero work order** - isolate and recalibrate Old Works routing;
choose public patrol telemetry or a temporary credential. (design 7.5)

**Settlement** - one plurality result at season close; ties leave the
world unchanged. Personal votes and dissent remain recorded. (design 13.2)

**Sump work order** - restore a pump bypass and allocate its spare
power to a dry shortcut or a salvage niche. (design section 7.5)
