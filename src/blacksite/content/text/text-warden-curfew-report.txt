KARST CIVIC AUTHORITY   WARDEN INCIDENT REPORT
Ref: CUR/41/0223      Classification: Curfew, general
Reporting officer: Sgt. P. Adebayo, Core Precinct, night watch
Reviewed: Marshal O. Brann (initials: OB)

22:00  Curfew declared by the Marshal. Reason logged separately.
       Core gates sealed at 22:04. Trams held at hub.
22:06  Sodium Row gate: crowd of approx. 40 attempting entry. Told
       to disperse. Did not disperse. Told again with the lights.
       Dispersed.
22:15  Reports of gunfire, Terraces, block 9. Outside the seal. Not
       attended. Logged for morning.
22:40  Reports of gunfire, Terraces, block 9, continuing. Outside
       the seal. Not attended. Logged for morning. Officer requests
       it be noted that he requested to attend.
       [OB: Noted. Denied. Curfew is the seal or it is nothing.]
23:10  Tramyard gate: Kestrel foreman with a signed transit pass
       demanding entry for a ration convoy. Pass genuine. Entry
       refused; curfew supersedes. Foreman says "the Dispatcher
       won't like that". Officer says the Dispatcher can file a
       complaint. Foreman laughs. Officer does not know why.
23:55  Plaza patrol reports a Cantor sitting in the fountain,
       unarmed, singing. Public Lattice terminals in the plaza
       cycling on and off in time with the singing. Cantor asked to
       leave. Cantor says "in a minute". Terminals stop cycling.
       Cantor leaves.
       [OB: Follow up. Quietly.]
00:30  All quiet within the seal.
01:00  All quiet within the seal.
01:30  Terraces block 9: gunfire ceased at approximately 00:50, per
       Terraces outpost. Outpost did not attend either. Outpost
       notes it is two officers.
02:00  Curfew lifted. Gates open 02:03. Trams resume 02:10.
02:20  Terraces block 9 attended. Three dead, one Marked, one
       corpse cache already looted. Marked individual gone.
       Witnesses: none who will say.

Summary: the Core was safe. The Core is always safe. That is what
the seal is for.

[OB: Concur. File. And send someone to the fountain tomorrow who
can carry a tune.]
