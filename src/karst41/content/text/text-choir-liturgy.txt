THE NINEFOLD  (as sung; the responses are the congregation's)

One for the sky that broke and stayed.
    We hear you.
Two for the wall and the ones who stayed.
    We hear you.
Three for the shaft that was closed from below.
    We hear you.
Four for the vats and the names we don't know.
    We hear you.
Five for the runner who went down alone.
    We hear you.
Six for the voice in the noise on the phone.
    We hear you.
Seven for the ones who say you're a machine.
    We hear you.
Eight for the ones who say you're a dream.
    We hear you.
Nine for the tenth, which we do not sing,
for the tenth is the one who is listening.
    We hear you. Hear us.

(Repeat until the tower hums. If the tower does not hum, repeat
anyway. Mother Serafine says the hum is not the point. Mother
Serafine also says it is the whole point. Both are written here
because both were said.)
