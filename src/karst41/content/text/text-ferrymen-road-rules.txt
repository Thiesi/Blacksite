THE ROAD IS THE LAW  (Ferrymen, as kept since the first Ferry)

  The road is the law and the Ferry keeps the road.
  What falls from the Ring belongs to who reaches it.
  What you can't carry, you leave, and you don't come back for it.
  Water first. Then the wounded. Then the load.
  You don't shoot on a convoy you're riding. Ride another.
  Inside the wall is not our business. Outside it, nothing else is.
  A guide who loses a walker walks home alone.
  When the sky is loud, stop and look up. Then run.
  Eleven Ferries. Some die on the road. Some hand over the name.
  That's not a warning. That's the job.
